= Hello, OpenShellOrg
:revdate: 2026-07-25
:description: Why this organization exists, and what we are building first.

OpenShellOrg is a home for **standards and companion tooling** that make shells and CLIs less chaotic — for humans writing commands and for machines piping data.

== The short version

We care about three things:

. Predictable CLI flag syntax (**SOS** — Save Our Syntax)
. Structured shell pipelines (we endorse https://www.nushell.sh/[Nushell]; we do not reinvent it)
. Progressive help that does not dump a wall of text

If you landed here looking for https://github.com/NVIDIA/OpenShell[NVIDIA OpenShell]: different project.
That one is an agent sandbox runtime.
This one is about CLI and shell DevX.
