Hello, OpenShellOrg

Why this organization exists, and what we are building first.

OpenShellOrg is a home for standards and companion tooling that make shells and CLIs less chaotic — for humans writing commands and for machines piping data.

The short version

We care about three things:

  1. Predictable CLI flag syntax (SOS — Save Our Syntax)

  2. Structured shell pipelines (we endorse Nushell; we do not reinvent it)

  3. Progressive help that does not dump a wall of text

If you landed here looking for NVIDIA OpenShell: different project. That one is an agent sandbox runtime. This one is about CLI and shell DevX.
